Steam chemical cleaning vs normal aircon cleaning

Both services have a place. The right choice depends on the unit condition, symptoms and maintenance history.

Short answer
Standard servicing is suitable for routine upkeep of a clean unit. Steam chemical cleaning is intended for visible internal buildup, recurring odour or restricted airflow.
